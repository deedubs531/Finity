# Finity Manifesto

Finity is a tool I build for myself to take back my attention, my mood and my money
from social media that is designed to keep me scrolling.

## Why this exists

Modern feeds are optimized for time-on-app, not for the person using them. They
use social comparison, infinite scroll, variable-reward ("slot machine") mechanics,
surveillance-based advertising and personalized pricing. These cost me time,
self-worth and money.

Finity keeps the parts of social media that are worth having and drops the rest.

## What I want back

1. **Inspiration.** Ideas, art and people that make me want to create or do something.
2. **Contentment.** Leaving a session feeling okay about my own life, not measured against everyone else's highlight reel.
3. **Connection.** Real contact with people I actually know or care about.
4. **Local awareness.** Knowing about events, shows and gatherings near me that I'd be excited to attend.

## What Finity refuses to do

| Harmful pattern | Finity's alternative |
|---|---|
| Infinite scroll | Finite feeds with a clear end ("You're caught up") |
| Autoplay and endless reels | Nothing plays unless I choose it; no auto-advance |
| Variable rewards (pull-to-refresh, random drops) | Content arrives on a predictable schedule I set |
| Public like counts and follower counts | Hidden by default; no rankings of people |
| Engagement-ranked algorithms | Chronological, or ranked by rules I can read and change |
| Streaks, badges and FOMO notifications | No streaks; notifications only for things I opted into |
| Personalized ads and pricing | No ads, no tracking, no profiling for sale |

## Data principles

- **Local-first.** My data stays on my device unless I choose to sync it.
- **Minimum collection.** Finity collects only what a feature needs to work.
- **No selling, no sharing, no ad SDKs, no third-party analytics.**
- **Exportable and deletable.** I can export or wipe everything at any time.
- **Transparent.** The code is open, so anyone can check what it does.

## Staying within the rules

Finity protects its user. It must not attack, deceive or break the platforms it
works alongside. Guardrails:

- **Use official, open or user-permitted data sources:** RSS, open protocols
  (Bluesky/AT Protocol, Mastodon/ActivityPub), official APIs within their terms,
  public event listings that permit reuse, and data the user exports themselves.
- **No scraping, no unofficial APIs, no automated logins** to platforms whose terms
  forbid it (this includes Instagram, TikTok, Facebook and X).
- **No storing or handling other people's passwords** for third-party services.
- **No bypassing paywalls, DRM, rate limits or security measures.**
- **No redistributing other people's content** beyond what its source allows; link back to the original.
- **Use platform wellbeing APIs as intended:** iOS Screen Time / Family Controls
  and Android Digital Wellbeing / usage-stats permissions, following each app
  store's policy (Android Accessibility Services in particular are tightly
  restricted on Google Play).
- **Browser-extension changes affect only what I see on my own screen.**
  They never automate actions on my account or anyone else's.
- **Nothing deceptive:** no impersonating platforms, no fake accounts, no manipulation of other users.

## How I'll know it's working

- I spend less time scrolling and decide for myself when to stop.
- I leave sessions feeling better, not worse.
- I see the people I care about more often, online and in person.
- I go to more local events.
- I spend less on impulse purchases driven by ads.

## The rule for every feature

> Does this help me live my life, or does it keep me inside the app?
> If it's the second, it doesn't ship.
